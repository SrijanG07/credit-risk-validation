Validation decision

boosting was selected by validation ROC-AUC before computing test metrics. Both fixed model specifications are reported. PR-AUC here means average precision (AP), not trapezoidal PR integration. Lower Brier, log loss and ECE are better.

roc_auc pr_auc_ap ks brier log_loss ece_10
logistic 0.7223 0.5043 0.3746 0.1439 0.4641 0.0469
boosting 0.7795 0.5574 0.4355 0.1327 0.4247 0.0114

Research benchmark only. This is a random, duplicate-grouped holdout from one historical cohort, not a temporal or external validation. It does not establish suitability for lending, Citi deployment, CCAR or CECL.

Leakage controls & uncertainty

Client ID, target and demographics are excluded from predictors. Identical predictor vectors remain in the same partition. Preprocessing is fit inside the training pipeline. Boosting uses sigmoid calibration with three grouped training folds. The validation set selects the model; the test set is not used to tune it.

AUC intervals use 300 cluster-bootstrap samples of held-out predictor groups. These intervals condition on the trained model and do not capture training instability or future population shift. No decision threshold or business cost assumption is optimized.

Monitoring laboratory

Reference = validation cohort. Current = held-out cohort. Synthetic adverse scenario reduces credit limits by 30% and adds one to repayment-status codes (capped at 9). It is a feature perturbation, not an economic forecast. No outcome metric is computed for invented future labels.

PSI uses reference-fitted quantile bins, infinite tails, a missing bin and 0.5 pseudocounts. Review values above 0.10; prioritize values above 0.25. These are configurable-style investigation heuristics, not regulatory or significance thresholds.

Limitations & next review

  • Historical Taiwanese credit-card clients do not represent current US lending or new applicants.
  • Labels concern next-month default; features are past account behavior. This is behavioral scoring, not an origination scorecard.
  • Sex and age subgroup diagnostics are supplied separately; excluding demographics does not eliminate proxies or demonstrate fairness.
  • Obtain newer, independently sourced temporal cohorts and business costs before considering operational use.
